# Math/physics audit

## Summary (5 lines max)

Arithmetic across the vault is unusually clean: I independently recomputed ~60 numbers (C1, C3, C4 §0/§5/§8/§11.4, C6 all 14 table rows, C11 §1–2, C12 §6, C19 §3–4, C21 Hill numbers) and every one reproduced to the quoted digits — the OLS fits in C4 §11.4 (b = 1.13 ± 0.40 and b = 1.20 ± 0.12) and the C19 divided-difference quadratic reproduce exactly.
The defects are almost never arithmetic; they are **physics-model** and **overclaim** defects, concentrated in the step between a correct calculation and the sentence in the pull-quote.
Highest-value hits: C11's `F_req ≈ 1–9 mN` omits the Oberth factor `V∞/V_p` and is ~1.9× too large (correct: 0.5–4.9 mN), and its "Q ≈ 3 C" is 3 orders wrong; C8's `Σ_sail = 2v/c` contradicts its own definition (which gives `v/c`) and its `Σ_drag ≤ 1/3` bound does not exist under its own definition; C19's headline "≥15× window" is the ratio of the extreme *tested* doses, not a measured window.
C5 is the strongest note in the vault and I found no derivation error in it — the flagged issues are provenance and one unproved inequality the note already flags.
C1 is the weakest: three headline numbers, zero sources, zero derivation, and one of them (trabecular 0.939) is contradicted by C6's own re-derivation (0.726–0.952).

## Findings

- [C11] **HIGH** — `F_req` is computed from `m·ΔV∞` but `ΔV∞` is a change in *hyperbolic excess* speed, not the perigee impulse; the Oberth factor `V∞/V_p` is missing — evidence: note gives `Δp = 730 × 0.01346 = 9.83 kg m/s → F_req = 9.06 mN` over `τ_peri = 1085 s`. But `ΔE = m V∞ ΔV∞ = 6.73e4 J` and `ΔE = m V_p Δv_p` ⟹ `Δv_p = 6.73e4/(730 × 12739) = 7.24 mm/s`, i.e. `Δp = 5.29 kg m/s` and `F_req(peri) = 4.87 mN`, `F_req(10⁴ s) = 0.53 mN`. Recomputed spec is **0.5–4.9 mN**, not 1–9 mN — proposed fix: recompute the headline specification as `F ≈ 0.5–5 mN` and add one line deriving `Δv_p = (V∞/V_p)·ΔV∞`; all `A` values improve by the same 1.86×, so no exclusion flips.
- [C11] **HIGH** — the required spacecraft charge is stated as ~3 C and "10 orders of magnitude" beyond a 1 m body; both are wrong — evidence: `Q_req = F_req/(V_p B) = 1e-3/(12739 × 2.43e-5) = 3.2×10⁻³ C`, i.e. **3.2 mC**, and against the assumed floating `Q ~ 10⁻⁹ C` that is **6.5 orders**, exactly consistent with the note's own `A ≈ 3×10⁶` — proposed fix: replace "Q ≈ 3 C … by ~10 orders" with "Q ≈ 3 mC … by ~6.5 orders", which is self-consistent with `A`.
- [C8] **HIGH** — `Σ_sail = 2v/c` does not follow from the note's own definition `Σ = P_useful/(F·Δu)` with `Δu = c`, which gives `Σ = Fv/(Fc) = v/c` — evidence: the note's own IKAROS row computes `Σ = 33.6/2.67e5 = 1.3×10⁻⁴ ≈ v/c` (v/c = 1.0e-4), then claims "closed form Σ = 2v/c = 2×10⁻⁴ agrees", a 60% mismatch presented as agreement — proposed fix: state `Σ_sail = v/c`, and either drop the factor 2 or derive it explicitly from the reflected-photon energy balance; correct the "agrees" sentence to a quantitative comparison.
- [C8] **HIGH** — "`Σ_drag = V/Δu`, **max 1/3** at `V = Δu/3`" is a conflation of the power-coefficient optimum with a bound on Σ; under the note's own definition Σ is monotone increasing in V with supremum 1 — evidence: `Σ = F·V/(F·Δu) = V/Δu`, no stationary point; the classical `V = Δu/3` maximises `P = F V ∝ (Δu−V)²V` (Cp = 4/27), a different objective — proposed fix: delete "max 1/3" from the table, replace with "Σ = V/Δu ∈ [0,1]; the classical drag-machine *power* optimum sits at Σ = 1/3, which is not a bound on Σ". This removes one of the four "known bounds Σ recovers", so §5 item 1 must be weakened from four to three.
