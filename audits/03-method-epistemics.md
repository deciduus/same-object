# Method/epistemics audit

## Summary (5 lines max)

The method is unusually self-critical and already carries most of the standard LBD caveats, but its central instrument — citation intersection — is reported as raw counts with no null model, so "0 co-citers" is not yet calibrated against field size, database coverage, or terminology drift.
M1–M6 are post-hoc taxonomic labels, not falsifiable patterns; only M6 has been made operational (C20), and only there does it emit a checkable output.
The specification instruments are genuinely well-validated on positive controls but have **no negative control**: by construction they always return a specification, and no case has been run where the correct answer is "nothing to specify."
Selection bias is honestly named in exactly one place (Q7/C16) and is under-treated everywhere else; the "novelty" grades are conditional on ~10–15 searches per note and the audit says so, which is the right posture but caps every NOVEL grade at "unfalsified."
METHOD.md has drifted badly out of sync with vault/ (retired verdict vocabulary, superseded verdicts, a stale 13σ, five-vs-six moves), and the disclosure ledger contradicts its own tier totals.

## Findings
