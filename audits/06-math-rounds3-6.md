## Summary (5 lines)

The pattern audit 01 found persists and has one new wrinkle: the arithmetic is again almost clean — I independently re-derived C25's HJB, singular arc, index, both limits, eq. (4) and eq. (5); C27's `H = 4^(β−1)` and all eleven LBNL median/mean back-fits; C28's full PPV/p*/inverse-specificity grid; C30's `S_req` in kg/s, t/yr and mol/yr and the 1.17 MHz / 1.32 km/s line split; C31's `A = Ha/(Ha+r)` and `A_circ = r·y` from the three-state chain; and C29/C32's censored-Weibull profile likelihood — and every one reproduced.
But this round the defects are **not only in the pull-quote**: three are hard numeric errors inside the body. C29 §5.3's `h(40)/h(1) = 40^(−0.413)` is **0.218, not 0.30**, so "three times less promising" should be ~4.6×. C30 row 10 equates Bains' extremal bound `1.3×10⁵ cm⁻² s⁻¹` with `800 t/yr`; it is **1,066 t/yr**, so the volcanic `A` is **10.7, not 8.0** — above F7's `A < 10` band, which dissolves divergence D1, the note's headline "one row diverges, against Bains". C25's `t*` column assumes arrival at `x = 1` while the model's own passive dynamics say arrival is at `1 − (1−GUD)e^{−rτ}`; the self-consistent residence ratio at `r·τ = 0.2` is **0.198, not 0.757**.
Highest-value overclaims: C28's pull-quote cites "the specificities the field's own false-positive enumeration can plausibly support (0.90–0.99)" against a body that proves specificity is *not estimable at all*; C32 reports pooled `β = 0.733` as what "is not damaged" while its own §5 shows the correct current-status likelihood degenerates that same data to `β = 0.051`; C27 §5 calls a θ=8-yr vs θ=0 fit pair a passing "natural experiment" three sections after §6(c) rules those two rows non-comparable.
C30 row 6 is a units error, not a rounding one: Bains' "8–15 orders of magnitude" is an **oxygen-fugacity** excess, tabulated as a **flux ratio** `A = 10⁸–10¹⁵` — the widest margin on the ledger and the anchor of §6's "largest robust abiotic A is 10¹⁵".
C26 is the strongest note of the eight: its negative result is honestly framed, its controls are genuinely matched on window and detrending, and I found no derivation error — only a too-generous null (φ fitted on the transition-containing residual) and two CI types tabulated under one heading.

## Findings

- [C30] **HIGH** — row 10's `S_req` at Bains' extremal aperture is wrong by 33%, and the correction removes the note's only claimed divergence from Bains — evidence: the row states "bound falls to `1.3×10⁵ cm⁻² s⁻¹` = 800 t/yr" and `volcanic A → 8.0`. `1.3×10⁵ cm⁻² s⁻¹` over Venus' `4.602×10¹⁸ cm²` is **1,066 t/yr**, so `A = 1,066/100 = 10.7`. The 800 t/yr figure is `8×10⁵/1000`, i.e. a round `τ×10³`, while `_scripts/c30_phosphine.py` **itself prints** `Bains' extremal-aperture lower bound: 1.3e+05 cm-2 s-1 (ratio 769x)` and then ignores it. `A = 10.7` sits **outside** `[[reservoir-audit]]` F7's `1 < A < 10` band, so the row is `RULED OUT`, D1 collapses, and the pull-quote's "**One row diverges, against Bains**" and §6's "(ii) the mandatory aperture sensitivity … shows the volcanic exclusion is not aperture-robust" both fall — proposed fix: recompute row 10 from `1.3×10⁵ cm⁻² s⁻¹` (script already has the 769× number), report `A = 10.7`, and either withdraw D1 or restate it as "sits on the F7 boundary at 10.7 and is aperture-fragile", which is a much weaker and honest claim.

- [C25] **HIGH** — §3's "independent confirmation" via `V'(x) = 1−x` is circular, and the shadow-price story it licenses is not derivable — evidence: `V' = 1−x` is obtained by substituting `ν = W(x)` into (2) *at every* `x`, i.e. by asserting indifference everywhere; that is the definition of the Whittle index, not a check on it. `V(x) = x − x²/2` does not solve the HJB with a constant gain: the active branch gives `λx − λx(1−x) = λx²`, which varies with `x` and so cannot equal a constant `ρ`. Each `ν` has its own value function; there is no single `V` across the family. §3's "Substituting `V' = 1−x` back into (1) reproduces (3) identically" is therefore trivially true and confirms nothing — proposed fix: delete "independent confirmation", state that `V' = 1−x` is the indifference condition rewritten, and drop or hedge the "room left to grow" interpretation, which currently reads as a derived result.
