# Manufacture contrast

> When the signal sits under the noise floor, do not amplify it. **Create a local disparity that survives the floor.**

A plant cell cannot feel its own weight — gravity loses to surface forces at that scale. So
it grows a dense starch grain whose displacement costs only **2–3 k_BT**, marginally above
thermal noise. A stack of five to ten clears it comfortably.

LIGO cannot make the gravitational wave bigger, so it squeezes the vacuum and **relocates the
noise** into the quadrature it is not measuring.

belongs to, and [[G11-plant-gravisensing]] for the statolith case.
