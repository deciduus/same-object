# Work inside the noise

> Three postures toward noise, and **each community knows one.**

- **Suppress** — cryogenics, shielding, isolation, averaging. Engineering's default.
- **Exploit** — [[M2-use-the-noise]]. Biology's, repeatedly.
- **Redistribute** — squeezing. LIGO moves uncertainty out of the measured quadrature. As far
  as this survey found, biology has no analogue.

## The structural point, which may matter more than the postures

**Biology measures from inside. We measure from outside.**

A shark reads nanovolt-per-centimetre fields because it is *immersed in* the field, sampling
across a 10 cm baseline of its own body in a conductive medium. It never fights distance.

We measure brains through skulls and gravity across gaps, paying 1/r^3 for a dipole field and
1/r^7 for force on neutral matter.

That is structural advantage, not cleverness — and it may explain more of the apparent gap
than any design insight. Practical rule: **when a measurement is falloff-limited, get closer
rather than more sensitive.**
