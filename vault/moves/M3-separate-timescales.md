# Separate the timescales

> Do not build a faster actuator. **Decouple slow loading from fast release** and let an instability do the work.

Muscle tops out near 500 W/kg. Latch-mediated spring actuation beats it by orders of
magnitude — trap-jaw ants reach ~10^5 g, closing mandibles at 64 m/s in 0.13 ms.

The fern sporangium goes further and needs **no crossbar**: fast elastic recoil and slow
viscous return differ enough to act as a purely dissipative escapement. Launch acceleration
~6300 g, and the energy is stored by **evaporation** — the sun does the loading, at zero
metabolic cost.

The open measurement is [[G12-latch-fatigue]]. See also [[LaMSA-latch]].
