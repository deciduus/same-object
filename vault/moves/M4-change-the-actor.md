# Change the actor

> When a theorem or scaling law blocks the direct route, **swap the category** of the thing doing the work.

A mechanical mirror cannot move fast enough for the dynamical Casimir effect — about one
photon per day at 2 GHz and 1 nm amplitude. Make the boundary a boundary *condition* with no
mass, in a superconducting circuit, and win by **twelve orders of magnitude**.

You cannot spin a medium fast enough for Zel'dovich amplification, so modulate it in time and
obtain effective superluminal rotation.

Broadband passive invisibility is **forbidden** by Bode–Fano matching theory. The escape is
to break time-invariance — which turns out to be the same door as vacuum particle creation.
