# Use the noise

> The engineering reflex is suppression. Repeatedly wrong — efficiency is often **non-monotonic** in noise, with an optimum well above zero.

Zero noise traps excitations in destructive-interference dark states; too much freezes them
by the Zeno effect. There is an optimum between, and biological reorganization energies sit
near it. Confirmed on trapped ions, photonic waveguides and programmable nanophotonics.

The statolith pile would **jam** below a yield angle if the cell were quiet. Actomyosin
agitation fluidizes it into a working angle sensor.

Honest correction: engineering does this too, in **dithering** — deliberately adding noise to
beat an ADC's resolution. Standard practice, and nobody calls it a principle.

The one case where the cross-domain synthesis *was* written: stochastic resonance, discovered
in climate science ~1981, found in crayfish and paddlefish, unified in a *Reviews of Modern
Physics* article in 1998. See [[positive-controls]].
