# Vary what was held fixed

> A hard bound is rarely wrong. What is usually wrong is **the list of things its derivation held fixed without saying so.**

The historical record favours this over "the constant is wrong":

| Case | The constant survived | The neighbour was free |
|---|---|---|
| Dysprosium-163 | nuclear stability | the **electron environment** — stable neutral, 47-day half-life fully stripped |
| Rhenium-187 | beta decay physics | the **available final state** — 4.2e10 yr to 32.9 yr |
| Solar neutrinos | the solar model | **flavour** |
| Muon g−2 | the measurement | the **hadronic input** |
| Continental drift | the geology | **seafloor spreading** |

## The procedure

1. Write down what the derivation **assumed**, not what it concluded.
2. Ask which assumptions were varied experimentally and which were merely inherited.
3. The unexamined ones are the work.

## Now a computable operator: [[C20-release-the-constant]]

For any bound expressed dimensionally, M6 is an explicit operation on the dimension matrix.
**"Release the constant":** a capability is constant-bound when a Π-group carries a fixed
constant (`kT`, `g`, `c`, `w`) that no free parameter can compensate. The operator **adds a
column** — a new physical quantity carrying the constant's signature — so the constant re-enters
a *tunable* ratio. It emits the **dimensional signature the new quantity must have.**

**It re-derives gecko contact-splitting blind.** The gecko matrix gives strength
`σ_c ∝ (Ew/R)^{1/2}`; with one contact `R ∝ √A`, so `σ_c ∝ A^{−1/4}` — the known anti-scaling.
The surface-energy constant `w` is locked to the scale `R`. The operator emits *"add a length
independent of A,"* and the sub-contact radius `a` is exactly that — recovering the
Arzt–Gorb–Spolenak `√n` law. **The known biomimetic fix falls out of the integer algebra**, run
blind on the matrix.

**And it points at active drive for a kT wall.** `E_sig/kT` has one lever (raise the signal).
Add an independent drive energy `U_drive` and a second group `U_drive/kT` appears — the
equilibrium floor becomes a tunable `kT_eff`, the active-bath escape of [[G11-plant-gravisensing]].

**The honest limit:** the operator returns the *dimension* a solution must have, never that a
physical quantity with that dimension exists. A group that is a ratio of two fixed constants
(Schmidt `ν/D`, C12's locked case) is a **real impossibility**; a group with an available
signature but no known realisation is a **lead, not a result.** That distinction makes
[[G22-scale-transfer-triage]]'s constant-bound criterion an actionable, finite search — which
G22 said no one had built.

## Worked on this project's own hardest wall

No matter-excluding barrier projects at standoff — force on neutral matter falls as **1/r^7**.
That derivation assumes four things:

| Held fixed | Varied? |
|---|---|
| matter is neutral | yes |
| free space, no cavity | yes — this is why cavities work |
| ambient temperature | yes — this is flux pinning |
| **the field is static in time** | **open** |

And the fastest-moving physics sits exactly there: photonic time crystals, Floquet
rotational superradiance, circuit dynamical Casimir. Not proof the wall opens — a
better-aimed question than "make the field stronger.
