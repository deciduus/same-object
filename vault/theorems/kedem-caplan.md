# Kedem-Caplan degree of coupling

> Works for any two conjugate flux-force pairs. **Thermoelectric ZT is algebraically a special case:** q^2 = ZT/(1+ZT).

Papers citing both it and the record thermoelectric material: **2**.

The unification is therefore not speculative — it is *proven possible* for at least one pair
in the class, and nobody has extended it.

**Limit:** linear-response only. It cannot reach dynamic soaring (nonlinear,
trajectory-dependent) or solar sails (no conjugate flux pair). Extending it past linear
response is the open problem in [[G1-gradient-coupling]].
