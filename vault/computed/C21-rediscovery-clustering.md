# Do the rediscovered objects concentrate? A count against the Q10 frame

> **Verdict: NO — not beyond what the method's own selection filter forces.** Enumerating
> every independent-rediscovery and every established cross-domain bridge in the vault gives
> **18 instances spread across 11 distinct mathematical object-types**, with an **effective
> number of object-types (inverse-Simpson / Hill N2) ≈ 9.0** (Hill N1 ≈ 9.9) — i.e. the
> distribution is close to *uniform*, the opposite of the tight clustering
> [[Q10-substrate-independent-logic]] predicted. The top two types absorb only **33%** of
> instances, the top three **44%**. There is a *coarser* reading in which the six object-types
> the frame pre-named (Möbius, integer-lattice, optimal-stopping, kT·ln2 ledger, reliability
> distribution, Onsager bilinear) absorb **72%** of instances — but that reading **is the
> selection confound made visible**, not evidence against it: the method is built to retrieve
> exactly those forms, and the only apparent concentration (Weibull ×3, index-policy ×3) is the
> *project reusing its own favourite lenses across several gaps*, not the world rediscovering
> one object. The single bias-controlled sample of genuinely independent rediscoveries — the
> [[Q2-independent-rediscovery]] table, n = 6 — scatters **one-per-type across five types**.
> **The strong "one substrate-independent logic" reading is not supported by the count. What
> survives is a weaker, real claim about the project's *method*, below.**
>
> *Self-reference, as the brief demanded it be named:* the concentration is measured with the
> inverse-Simpson / Hill number, which is *itself one of the project's recurring objects*
> ([[hill-number-multifunctionality]]). It does not privilege its own family — it returns
> Hill N2 ≈ 9, reporting low concentration of the very objects it belongs to.

**Top-types fraction:** top-2 = 6/18 = **33%**; top-3 = 8/18 = **44%**. Neither is "most."

**Effective number of object-types (the project's own tool, [[hill-number-multifunctionality]]):**

- Simpson `Σpᵢ² = (9+9+4+4+4+1+1+1+1+1+1)/18² = 36/324 = 0.1111`
  → **Hill N2 = inverse-Simpson = 1/0.1111 = 9.0**
- Shannon `H = −Σ pᵢ ln pᵢ = 2.293` → **Hill N1 = exp(H) = 9.9**

Observed richness is 11; the effective number is ≈ 9–10. **A distribution this flat is the
signature of *no* concentration** — near one instance per type. For contrast, real clustering
onto "a small set" would drive Hill N2 down toward 2–3.

## 3. The selection confound — stated prominently, and it is close to fatal to the strong reading

There is a coarser count that *looks* like clustering: the **six object-types the frame
pre-named** (A Möbius, B/H lattice, C stopping, D ledger, E reliability, F Onsager) absorb
instances 1–13 = **13/18 = 72%**, leaving only G, I, J, K (5/18 = 28%) outside. A reader wanting
the frame to have teeth would quote the 72%.

**That reading is the confound, not a refutation of it.** Three reasons the 72% is manufactured:

1. **The net is shaped to catch these forms.** [[specification-instruments]] *defines* the
   method as "an exact accounting identity in a conserved quantity + a dimensionless bridge."
   The mathematical objects in which such an identity can be *written* are precisely Möbius
   figures of merit, Onsager bilinears, and integer-kernel dimensionless groups. Finding that
   instances land there is finding that the sieve retrieves sieve-shaped things. METHOD §1 says
   this in advance: "once you are looking for unifying principles, you find them everywhere."

2. **The only real concentration (C=3, E=3) is the analyst reusing one lens, not the world
   rediscovering one object.** E's three are all the *same* object (Weibull / stress-strength
   interference) applied by the project to three different gaps (C18, C2, positive-controls).
   C's three are Charnov=Gittins, Green=stopping, Gittins=RL — facets the *project itself*
   linked. The common citer in these "clusters" is this vault. Collapse each reused lens to one
   object and C→1, E→1, and the distribution flattens further, toward Hill ≈ 11.

3. **The bias-controlled subsample does not cluster.** The [[Q2-independent-rediscovery]] table
   is the only set assembled *before* an object-type taxonomy existed — six genuinely
   independent rediscoveries. They fall in **five different types**: F (Onsager, #1), E
   (stress-strength, #10), C (stopping, #4 and Charnov=Gittins #3), H (passivity lattice, #13),
   B (Péclet, #9). One-per-type, not a small set. If independent rediscoveries clustered on a
   few objects, this is exactly the sample that would show it, and it shows the opposite.

**Can we distinguish "the world's rediscoveries cluster" from "we kept the ones that fit our
object-types"?** On this evidence, no — and the tie goes against the strong claim, because the
clustering visible in the full 18 is entirely accounted for by (1) the seeded type-list and (2)
lens reuse, while the one uncontaminated sample (Q2) is flat. There is no residual concentration
left for a substrate-independent logic to explain.

## 4. What the result can and cannot support

**Cannot support (the strong Q10 reading):** that independent rediscoveries in general
concentrate on a small set of recurring mathematical objects. Effective number of types ≈ 9 of
11; the controlled subsample is one-per-type; the apparent 72%-on-six-types is a selection
artifact of a method built to retrieve those six.

**Can support (a weaker, genuinely earned claim about the *method*, not about knowledge):** the
project's *specification instruments* converge on a small toolkit — integer kernels, Möbius
figures of merit, index policies, kT·ln2 ledgers, Weibull hazards — because those are the finite
set of forms in which "an exact conserved-quantity identity + a compensable dimensionless
bridge" can be expressed. That convergence is real, is already tabulated in
[[specification-instruments]], and explains why *this project's outputs* rhyme. It is a fact
about what makes a good specification instrument, **not** evidence that knowledge reduces to one
substrate-independent logic. The frame in [[Q10-substrate-independent-logic]] should be
demoted accordingly: it organises the project's *findings-selection* honestly, but its testable
edge — clustering of real rediscoveries — came back **null**.

**The honest null, reported gladly:** the rediscovered objects do **not** concentrate beyond
what the method's selection hunger already predicts. That is a real and useful finding: it means
Q10's lens is pattern-affinity of the instrument, not a discovered structure of knowledge — and
the sharpest single piece of counter-evidence is the project's own bias-controlled table
[[Q2-independent-rediscovery]], which scatters one object per type.
