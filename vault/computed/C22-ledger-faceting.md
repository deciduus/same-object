# C22 — Faceting the disclosure ledger and testing the access-basis prediction

> **Verdict on the central prediction (access basis -> confirmation).** *Directionally supported,
> not quantitatively established.* In the full 24-row corpus, firsthand-tagged entries confirm far
> more than secondhand/hearsay ones (12/22 = 55% vs 0/2 = 0%). But that gap is carried almost
> entirely by the **documentary lane**: 16 of 24 rows are institutional documents that "confirm"
> as *provenance* (they are primary records), which says nothing about the phenomenon. Restricted
> to the **witness-claim subset (effective N ~ 9)**, essentially nothing confirms at the referent
> level: the firsthand sightings (Fravor, Graves) sit in "acknowledged but officially unexplained,"
> and the secondhand-briefed (Grusch) and hearsay (Nell) claims are uncorroborated or pushed back
> on. What genuinely holds is the taxonomy's own worked contrast — **firsthand sensor-data got
> officially acknowledged; secondhand-briefed recovery/biologics claims stayed open or were
> refuted** — but with N this small and this documentary-heavy, treat it as **suggestive, not
> conclusive.** See [[disclosure-ledger]], [[testimony-taxonomy]], [[Q1-what-gets-checked]],
> [[evidence-lanes]].

## Method note

Every entry in [[disclosure-ledger]] faceted on the seven axes of [[testimony-taxonomy]]. Access
basis (facet 2) is coded as the *primary* basis of the row's claim:
**F-D** = firsthand-data / documentary, **F-O** = firsthand-observation,
**S-B** = secondhand-briefed, **H** = hearsay / bare assertion. No mechanism is asserted in the
project's voice; this is provenance/faceting only, to the ledger's discipline.

| **Secondhand+hearsay (S-B+H)** | **0** | **2** | **0** | **2** | **0%** |

**Read this honestly.** The 55%-vs-0% split is in the predicted direction, but three things gut it
as a quantitative result:

1. **The documentary lane dominates.** F-D holds 16 of 24 rows and 11 of 12 confirmations. Those
   confirm because they *are* primary records ([[evidence-lanes]]: the official/documentary lane's
   strength is provenance) — not because a phenomenon was established. A released report confirming
   "we released this report" is trivially confirmed.
2. **F-O actually confirms *less* than F-D** (17% vs 69%) — the opposite of a naive "firsthand is
   best" reading. Firsthand *observation* of UAP lands in "acknowledged but unexplained," which the
   coding scores as still-open, not confirmed. So within firsthand, the documentary sub-lane, not
   the eyewitness sub-lane, carries the confirmations.
3. **The secondhand cell is N=2.** A 0% rate on two rows is not a rate.

**In the witness subset, the referent confirms in zero of nine rows.** What separates the rows is
*not* confirmation rate (all uniformly unconfirmed) but **direction of movement**: the
secondhand/hearsay claims (7, 20) and the disputed-role claim (17) are the ones that drew active
pushback or refutation; the firsthand *observations* (8, 9) are logged and preserved as genuinely
open. This is exactly the taxonomy's worked example — Navy videos (firsthand data, documentary
lane, rows 2/3/14) acknowledged; recovered-hardware/biologics (secondhand-briefed, row 7) open and
contradicted. The prediction survives as a *qualitative* landscape claim, at the taxonomy's own
"specification, never mechanism" resolution — nothing here upgrades toward the phenomenon, because
no row meets the intersection cell (F-D + independent multi-witness/cross-sensor + verified +
checkable referent): rows 2/3/14 are F-D and cross-sensor but their *anomalous residual* is not
referent-checkable, and rows 8/9 are multi-witness firsthand but not firsthand-*data*.

- **16 Gallaudet (influence-op characterization)** — an account of a single private meeting with no

  classification, so uncheckable by outsiders.

Clean Q1 reading, matching [[disclosure-ledger]] §1: **the officially-unexplained sensor cases are
open because the confirming check is unfunded/expensive; the exotic-recovery claims are open
because they are structured to be uncheckable from outside — and where an insider check did run, it
came back negative.**

## Small-N caveat (stated as prominently as the finding)

Twenty-four rows is a small sample, and it is the wrong *shape* for a clean rate test: **16 of 24
are institutional-documentary**, which confirm as provenance and swamp any witness signal. The
**witness-claim effective N is ~9**, and the **secondhand+hearsay cell is N=2** — a 0% there is not
a measured rate, it is two data points. No significance is claimed and none should be read in.
Consistent with [[testimony-taxonomy]]'s selection-bias warning, the TESTIMONIAL rows are a
non-random, self-selected sample with no denominator (we cannot see the credentialed insiders who
saw nothing or stayed silent). The correct summary is the one in the verdict blockquote: the
access-basis pattern holds **directionally and as a landscape description** — firsthand sensor-data
got acknowledged, secondhand-briefed recovery claims did not — and is **suggestive, not
conclusive.** The referee remains convergence across orthogonally-biased lanes ([[evidence-lanes]]),
not a within-corpus correlation this thin.
