# Faceting credentialed accounts, so the pile yields structure

> The user's framing, and the right one: don't adjudicate cases — **sort them.** A corpus of
> credentialed testimony, faceted along the axes below, reveals structure in the *claims* that
> single-case contestation cannot touch. The discipline: structure in the testimony is data
> about the **reporting landscape**, and it upgrades toward the phenomenon only where the
> narrow condition in the last section is met.

## The facets

Every account gets tagged on all seven. The schema is designed so the entries in the
provenance ledger (`disclosure-ledger`) slot straight in.

| Facet | Values | Why it sorts |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Credential class** | aviator · intelligence officer · program/contractor insider · scientist/engineer · sensor operator · oversight/political | who the person is |
| **2. Access basis** | firsthand-observation · firsthand-data/documentary · secondhand-briefed · hearsay | **the single most diagnostic facet** — see below |
| **3. Claim object** | sighting kinematics · sensor / multi-spectral data · program existence · materials/craft retrieval · interface/display · biologics | what is being claimed |
| **4. Evidence tier** | documented · testimonial · attributed/refuted | from the ledger |
| **5. Corroboration** | independent multi-witness · cross-sensor · single-source | how many independent channels |
| **6. Credential verifiability** | verified · partial · contested | is the person who they say |
| **7. Confirmation trajectory** | confirmed · still-open · refuted · attributed (+ lag in years) | what happened since |

## Why access-basis (facet 2) is the spine

A credential says the person is not a random crank. It does **not** transfer to the claim. What
partly transfers is **access basis**: a firsthand sensor-operator reporting instrument data is a
different epistemic object from a program-insider relaying what they were *briefed*. The project
already has the rule — [[reservoir-audit]], testimony sets the specification — and access-basis
is how far a given specification is from the observable.

The predicted structure, to be tested against the ledger, not assumed:

> **Firsthand observational and firsthand-data claims should confirm at a higher rate than
> secondhand-briefed and hearsay claims.** The Navy videos (firsthand data) were acknowledged;
> the recovered-hardware/biologics claims (secondhand-briefed) remain open. If that pattern
> holds across the corpus, it is a real finding *about the landscape* — and it locates where the
> checkable signal is.

## The minable questions (the deliverable)

Once faceted, the pile answers questions no single case can:

1. **Does confirmation trajectory (7) correlate with access basis (2)?** The core test above.
2. **Which claim objects (3) have independent multi-witness or cross-sensor corroboration (5),
   and which are single-source?** Corroboration structure is more informative than any one
   witness's credential.
3. **[[Q1-what-gets-checked]] lens:** for the still-open claims, is the reason *unfalsifiable /
   unfundable to check*, or *checked and unresolved*? Q1 says the first is about the field's
   economics, not the claim's truth. Sort the open items by which.
4. **[[Q10-substrate-independent-logic]] lens — the strongest one:** do the recurring
   *observables* (kinematics like instantaneous acceleration; the [[symbol-display-testimony]]
   interface; low-observability) **cluster across independent firsthand accounts**, or diverge?
   Clustering of independent firsthand observables is the one pattern that carries weight;
   clustering of secondhand *narrative* mostly measures a shared story, not a shared thing.

## The one condition under which structure upgrades toward the phenomenon

A neat taxonomy of testimony is still testimony. It becomes evidence about the phenomenon only
where **facet 2 = firsthand-data, facet 5 = independent multi-witness or cross-sensor, facet 6 =
verified, and the observable is checkable** — the intersection cell. That cell is small, and it
is exactly where to concentrate any further inquiry. Everything outside it is a map of the
reporting landscape: real, sortable, worth having, and not to be mistaken for the territory.

This is [[Q10-substrate-independent-logic]]'s "do the objects concentrate?" test, pointed at
testimony instead of at mathematics — and held to the same standard.
