# What closes a gap

**The strongest single result of the project**, and it came from a candidate that came back
negative.

## The control case

Work-extracted-per-bit is **not** a gap. The metric — work over kT times mutual information —
is shared across colloidal, single-electron, cavity-QED and NV-centre platforms, which are
directly comparable despite sharing no hardware.

**Why?** Sagawa and Ueda gave it a **theorem** rather than a convention. Fix the denominator
and the shared figure of merit follows within a decade.

## The consequence

Every other gap on the list is missing exactly that. **Closing one does not require a review
article. It requires a theorem.**
