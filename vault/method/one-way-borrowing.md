# One-way borrowing

A real gap in the unread direction, and easy to mistake for a closed bridge. Field 3 of
[[relationship-description]].

## Cases

| Borrowing | Direction | Read back? |
|---|---|---|
| Ant colony optimisation | ants -> computer science | no |
| Information foraging theory | behavioural ecology -> human-computer interaction | no |
| Reliability theory of aging | engineering -> biology | **6 of 633 citers** |
| Optimal foraging into bandits | operations research -> its own framing | n/a |

## The lesson

**The export direction of a bridge tells you almost nothing about the import direction.**

A celebrated "successful biomimicry transfer" is entirely compatible with the source field
never reading the destination. Ant colony optimisation is cited thousands of times and ant
biology does not read it.

So a nonzero co-citation count needs a **direction** before it means anything — which is why
verdict scoring could not express these and [[relationship-description]] has a field for it.
