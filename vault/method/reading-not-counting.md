# Reading, not counting

> The `full-text-read` evidence grade. What it requires, and the case that forced it.

## Why the grade exists

[[citation-intersection]] is the strongest evidence here, but it answers only one question:
*do these two literatures touch?* It cannot tell you **what a paper says**. Two failures on
this project were invisible to counting:

1. A withdrawal cited a paper as a limits-to-sensing derivation. The paper was an experiment.
2. A claim carried a number that was wrong by two orders of magnitude.

Both sat in [[G11-plant-gravisensing]]. Neither is a citation-graph property. Only reading
found them.

## What the grade requires

All five. Anything less is `string-protocol`.

1. **Full text, not the abstract.** If only the abstract was available, say so in the note and
   the grade does not apply.
2. **Verbatim quotes** for anything load-bearing, with the fetch URL. Per the verification
   discipline in `METHOD.md` §4.
3. **ABSENT stated explicitly.** Not finding a thing must be recorded as a checked absence in
   a named source, never as silence.
4. **Arithmetic recomputed**, with a check against a number the source itself publishes. The
   G11 recomputation reproduced the paper's own Péclet number, which is what made it trustworthy.
5. **The symmetry rule.** A withdrawal must meet the standard the original claim would have
   had to meet. See [[relationship-description]].

## The failure it is designed to catch

Anchoring on the term the **originating** field uses instead of the **target** field's term —
the diagnosis from the audit, and the mechanism behind [[homographs]] and the synonym trap.
A string protocol inherits that anchor. A reader does not, because a reader sees what the
paper is actually about.

## Its own limit, stated

Reading is **selective**. You read the papers you found, and you found them with a search that
carries the same anchoring bias. `full-text-read` is therefore ranked **below**
[[citation-intersection]], not above it: a citation sweep is unbiased about which papers it
returns, and a reader is not.

The two grades fail differently, which is the argument for having both. The strongest position
is a claim that has survived each.
