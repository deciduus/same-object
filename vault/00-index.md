## Reading the standings

`standing` is a **closed vocabulary**: live, narrowed, withdrawn, overturned. `evidence` is
too, strongest first: **citation-intersection, full-text-read, string-protocol, single-review,
not-assessed.** Both are enforced by the lint. Prose lives in `note`, never in a machine field.

`full-text-read` means the primary sources were **read**, not counted — see
[[reading-not-counting]] for what the grade requires and why it ranks *below* citation
intersection. It caught two errors in one entry that no count could have:
[[G11-plant-gravisensing]].

**String-protocol findings have survived under half the time when re-tested by
[[citation-intersection]].** Weight accordingly.

## The instrument, and the antifragile move

The through-line the user named: **[[specification-instruments]]** — when you cannot predict the
unknown, build the exact identity that specifies it. Not a debunker, an antifragile tool that
returns a tighter specification the stranger the input. Its enumeration and exclusion steps are
discrete, and its cores keep turning out to be integer-lattice objects — the Gittins index, Hill
numbers, Buckingham Π-groups, citation intersection.

- [[specification-instruments]] — the template, and why it is antifragile rather than predictive

- [[C20-release-the-constant]] — **the move M6 as an integer operator.** It re-derives gecko contact-splitting *blind* from the dimension matrix, and makes [[G22-scale-transfer-triage]]'s triage an actionable, finite search

## The moves

Six structural patterns recurring across every field surveyed. None is *build a stronger thing*.

- [[M1-manufacture-contrast]] — signal under the noise floor? create a local disparity that survives it
- [[M2-use-the-noise]] — efficiency is often non-monotonic in noise, with an optimum above zero
- [[M3-separate-timescales]] — don't build a faster actuator; decouple slow load from fast release
- [[M4-change-the-actor]] — when a theorem blocks the route, swap the category of the thing doing the work
- [[M5-work-inside-the-noise]] — suppress, exploit, redistribute; each community knows one
- [[M6-vary-what-was-held-fixed]] — a bound is rarely wrong; the list of things its derivation silently held fixed usually is

## Computed

Not catalogue entries. Numbers this project produced.

- [[C25-whittle-foraging]] — **first Layer-3 derivation.** The Whittle index of a regrowing patch is `W(x) = λx² − r(1−x)²`; indexable unconditionally, reduces to C5 eq. (4) as r→0 under non-revisitability, and predicts giving-up density rising with regrowth rate (1.34× MVT at r·τ = 0.2). Dataset named: Kadmon & Shmida 1992. Five open holes stated
- [[C30-venus-phosphine-audit]] — the audit's first **step-0 halt**. `NO AGREED OBSERVABLE`: the 266.94 GHz feature's amplitude is a function of the reduction pipeline (20 ppb → 1–7 ppb → `<0.8 ppb` → `3 ppb at 5.7σ` from the same SOFIA photons). Run conditionally it excludes every abiotic PH₃ route at `A = 10²`–`10¹⁵`, matching Bains 2021 route-for-route, and declines one exclusion Bains asserts (volcanic, `A = 8.0` at Bains' own extremal aperture → `NOT TESTED` under F7). The biotic row `SURVIVES` the same ledger — a fact about the ledger, not about life
- [[C26-ews-hazard-shape]] — **an honest negative.** The early-warning indicator *can* be turned into a prognostics hazard, and the resulting Weibull `β` discriminates nothing: Cariaco 5.84 [1.98, 10.63] against a no-bifurcation surrogate at 7.39, `p = 0.66`. Corrects [[C18-durability-axis]] — `β` is estimator-dependent (4.41 vs 0.97 on the same 100 C-MAPSS units). What ecology needs from prognostics is the fleet, not the hazard model
- [[C27-product-lifespan-beta]] — 21 published product-lifespan Weibull fits on C18's β axis. Products span **β = 1.00–6.0** and split into a memoryless band (gas boilers, room ACs) and a wear-out band (cars, furnaces); the band, not the product category, is what a repair-vs-design-life policy should follow
- [[C28-biosignature-roc]] — **the base-rate term astrobiology does not have.** The O₂ biosignature as a diagnostic test: break-even prevalence `p* = (1−spec)/(sens+1−spec)`, so at 1-in-1,000 planets bearing life a detection needs specificity **≥ 0.999** to be more likely true than false. Six published abiotic-O₂ routes, **zero** published probabilities — specificity is not estimable from the literature, and that empty column is the finding
- [[C29-recovery-beta]] — **ecological recovery has a *decreasing* hazard.** Weibull β = 0.587 [0.510, 0.668] over 221 right-censored records from Jones & Schmitz 2009's own Table S1; β < 1 with the CI clear of 1 in 4 of 5 habitat classes. Opposite end of C18's axis from Li-ion wear-out. Recovery is early-or-never, not a clock. Mixture-vs-mechanism not separated, and the note says so
- [[C32-recovery-beta-replication]] — replication of C29's Weibull recovery β on Moreno-Mateos
- [[C31-remanufacturing-ha]] — Ha = L/T for a remanufactured product fleet; A = Ha/(Ha+r); the circular ceiling A_circ ≤ r
- [[C1-availability-living-tissue]] — photosystem II = 0.883, cortical bone = 0.984. **A leaf is less available than a power grid.**
- [[C2-probabilistic-safety-factors]] — for bone, failure probability is set by tissue variability, not locomotor variability
- [[C3-energy-error-axis]] — every information substrate on one kT-normalized axis
- [[C11-flyby-reservoir-audit]] — the audit's first open case. Every **static** reservoir excluded — the tempting geomagnetic one hardest, at A≈3×10⁶ — leaving a specification: 1–9 mN, along-track, scaling as 2ΩR⊕/c, non-stationary in epoch
- [[C10-healing-curve-fit]] — seven datasets fit. **Rate balance loses; a depleting envelope wins**, class-dependent. Confirms Kirkwood is the right frame and narrows Ha to the first cycle
- [[C9-moving-coupling-point]] — **a clean negative, and the register earned its keep.** The bound survives, the device is a Stirling engine with a bad regenerator, and a 2014 paper got there first. One calculation instead of a season in a workshop
- [[C8-momentum-harvesting-metric]] — **the founding question, answered.** Σ = P/(F·Δu), exact and bounded in [0,1] with no linearity assumed. Spans soaring, sails and tethers; reproduces the known minimum-shear condition as a check
- [[C7-discrepancy-catalogue]] — 39 cases. The headline fraction is **not** a base rate and the note says so; the finding that survives is a conditional, in [[Q7-same-class-prediction]]
- [[C5-charnov-gittins]] — **the strongest result in the project.** Charnov's rule *is* the Gittins index, as an identity in two lines. A 2024 paper re-derived it in foraging notation without ever naming Gittins
- [[C6-damage-healing-ratio]] — **Ha = k_r/k_d.** The missing object in [[G5-repair-number]], written down, with no prior art. Three systems on one axis; two rows left empty because the data do not exist
- [[C4-inclination-sensing-limit]] — **the derivation does not close at one cell.** It forces pooling; the existing ablation data cannot discriminate pooling from linear summation, because the discriminating angle was never measured. A ~500-root experiment would settle it
