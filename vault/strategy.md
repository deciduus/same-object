# Three layers, and where we work

> A strategic lens, distinct from the maturity ladder in `VISION.md`. That ladder is about how
> far a *single* finding has travelled (literature → computation → hypothesis → experiment).
> **This is about how far the whole inquiry is reaching**, and it sets the current focus.

## The layers

1. **Compare.** Take two literatures describing the same move, measure the contact, locate the
   gap, and see what would close it. Pure [[citation-intersection]] and full-text reading. The
   founding activity.
2. **Produce.** The loop where synthesis generates a *new resultant* — a bridge, a
   closed-by-construction object, a validated instrument. [[C5-charnov-gittins]],
   [[C8-momentum-harvesting-metric]], [[C12-pi-space-lattice]], the two audits. Not "here is a
   gap" but "here is the missing object, built."
3. **Deduce into the unknown.** Point a *validated* instrument at an open or contested case and
   read back the specification. [[C11-flyby-reservoir-audit]] was one careful step in; the
   information audit is loaded for it.

## Current focus: Layer 2, deliberately

**We stay in Layer 2 and lay groundwork before branching into Layer 3.** The reasons:

- Layer 2 is where the **method and the findings compound.** Each instrument built is reusable;
  each gap closed by construction is a permanent result, not a lead.
- **Doing more Layer 2 naturally widens Layers 1 and 2.** Building an object forces new
  comparisons and surfaces new gaps — C8 surfaced [[Q9-fuel-free-is-an-assumption]], C12 tied
  [[G21-dimensionless-regime-map]] to [[G22-scale-transfer-triage]]. Breadth is a by-product.
- **There is a lot of ground.** Gaps without a construction attempt yet; "unread theorem"
  claims that have never been citation-tested (and the one that was, [[kedem-caplan]],
  collapsed); moves not yet formalised. All of it is Layer 1–2.
- Layer 3 is real and reachable, but it spends the instruments rather than building them. It
  waits until the toolkit and the ground are wider.

## What Layer-2 groundwork looks like, concretely

- **Harden the headline claim.** The project's dominant pattern is "the unifying theorem sits
  unread." [[kedem-caplan]] was tested and was *not* unread. The other unread-theorem claims
  carry the same risk and have not been citation-tested. Test them.
- **Close more gaps by construction.** Where a gap names a missing object with a computable
  form — a figure of merit, a metric, a lattice — attempt to build it or prove it cannot exist,
  as [[C9-moving-coupling-point]] proved a device could not.
- **Formalise the discrete cores.** Several objects are integer-lattice or order-theoretic and
  have not been treated as such.
