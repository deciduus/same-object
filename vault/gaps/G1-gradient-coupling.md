# Gradient coupling has no shared figure of merit

> Dynamic soaring, solar sails, tethers, thermoelectrics, osmotic power, evaporation engines and chemiosmosis all extract work by coupling to a pre-existing gradient. **Thermoelectrics have ZT. Nothing spans the class.**

Calibration passed — `"dynamic soaring" AND "albatross"` returns 101, so the soaring side is
findable and its zeros mean something. Survived nine of ten alternate formulations.

## Topology

**Two clusters with no contact.** Momentum and field-gradient coupling (soaring, sails,
tethers) on one side; chemical and thermal gradient coupling on the other. Chemiosmosis sits on
a third, biochemical island.

## What is specifically absent

A figure of merit spanning the class. [[kedem-caplan]] **provably contains** thermoelectric ZT,
so the unification is not speculative — but it is linear-response only and cannot reach soaring
or sails.

## What would change it

Extend the degree of coupling past linear response. That is genuine open research, not an
application.
