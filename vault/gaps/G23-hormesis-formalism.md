# Hormesis has no engineering formalism

**STANDING: LIVE** · evidence: string-protocol · contact surface: 0 · last checked 2026-09-03

> Both fields now build stress-responsive strengthening. **Only one has a dose-response formalism for it.**

Both sides calibrate strongly — hormesis 901, mechanophore 547 — which is what makes the zeros
mean something. Every crossing **0**: shot peening, work hardening, strain hardening, cold
working, mechanophore, self-strengthening, stress-responsive material, training effect.

Meanwhile engineering demonstrably has the *phenomenon*: a 2021 polymer gel self-strengthens
under vibration with a **66x modulus increase**, localizing along the stress distribution and
explicitly framed as bone-like. Papers exist titled *Mechanochemical strengthening of a
synthetic polymer in response to typically destructive shear forces.*

**Stress-strengthening materials exist; nobody has written a dose-response curve for one.**

Biology's formalism to import: the ~30-60% amplitude ceiling and the 10-20x stimulatory window
width. Honest note — hormesis is genuinely contested in regulatory toxicology.
