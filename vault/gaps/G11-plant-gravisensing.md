# Limits to plant gravity sensing

> A single statolith displacement costs only **2-3 k_BT** — marginally above thermal noise.

## Status: withdrawn, and flagged for re-examination

Withdrawn as a **synonym artifact** — the literature says *gravisensing*, not *gravitropism*.
A paper titled *Noise amplification of plant gravisensing* computes statolith potential energy
against background thermal energy.

**But nobody read past the abstract.** A threshold calculation is not necessarily a
limits-to-sensing *analysis* in the Bialek sense — deriving what the physics permits, rather
than checking one number against kT. This is the third of three over-withdrawals identified when
the [[relationship-description]] symmetry rule was adopted, and the only one **not yet
re-examined properly.**

## Open work

Read the actual papers. Then either restore, narrow, or confirm the withdrawal.
