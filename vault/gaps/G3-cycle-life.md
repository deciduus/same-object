# No cycle-life framework

> Biology's catalytic-cycles-until-replacement, biocatalysis's total turnover number, battery cycle life and thermochemical cyclic stability are the same quantity under four names.

Every crossing 1-4 papers, all incidental. Both sides calibrate strongly.

**Where the neglect actually lives:** not batteries, where cycle life is a headline spec.
Thermochemical storage, where a celebrated result reads *"stable for at least ten cycles"* while
organic flow chemistries quote fade below **0.001% per cycle**.

Biology's numbers: enzymes run **10^3-10^7 turnovers** then are scrapped, usually killed by the
reaction they catalyse. The strategy is **a cheap catalyst plus a continuous replacement line**,
not a durable one. And ATP holds ~50 g against ~60 kg/day turnover — **the carrier is a bus,
not a tank.**
