# No metastability figure of merit

**STANDING: LIVE** · evidence: string-protocol · contact surface: 0 · last checked 2026-09-03

> Energetic materials, nuclear isomers, phase-change storage and biological dormancy quantify the same stability-versus-performance geometry in four vocabularies.

Every crossing zero or irrelevant on re-run; all four sides calibrate. The unifying language
(kinetic stability, activation barrier, kinetic trapping) is used **within** physics and
chemistry and is **absent from the biology side entirely** — 1 hit, irrelevant.

Governing arithmetic: lifetime goes as exp(dG/kT), so **every ~6 kJ/mol buys 10x lifetime.** One
year needs ~100 kJ/mol; a million years ~135. **Barrier grows logarithmically while energy
density is unbounded** — the tradeoff people assume is much weaker than it feels.

Homograph warning: "metastability" is owned by ADC circuit design and tumour dormancy. See
[[homographs]].
