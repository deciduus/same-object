Results that close a gap by supplying the missing object — a theorem or a metric — rather than merely locating it. The strongest currency the project deals in.

C5 · computedidentity

Charnov's rule is the Gittins index

Behavioural ecology's patch-leaving rule (1976) and operations research's bandit index (1979) are the same object, in two lines: Charnov's maximisation over residence time is literally the supremum over stopping times in the index definition.

ν(t) = g′(t) = R*  // leave when current index meets the alternative's

The travel time τ is neither a switching cost nor zero — it's a zero-reward prefix inside the outside arm, licensed by patches being non-revisitable. Both earlier guesses were half right.

Confirmed at the hardest standard: citation intersection 5 of 1,013 (0.49%) vs a 22.2% positive control — factor 45. A 2024 paper re-derived it in foraging notation with zero occurrences of “Gittins.”
C8 · computedfounding question

One figure of merit for fuel-free harvesting

Dynamic soaring, solar sails and tethers all extract work from a gradient with no onboard fuel and shared no metric. Now they do — from an exact identity, so it is bounded by construction, no linearity assumed.

Σ ≡ Puseful / (F · Δu)  ∈ [0,1]

It reproduces the soaring literature's minimum-shear condition from scratch as a check. And it split the class: a kinematic half where only magnitude helps (photon sail = 2v/c), and a trajectory half where arrangement is the only lever. That's the founding intuition made into a criterion.

Corrected our own physics: the note claimed a sail has no conjugate pair. False — its partner is the radiation field, Δu = c.
reservoir-audit · methodvalidated 5/5

A reservoir audit that specifies, never verdicts

Σ inverts into a tool: a measured value above 1 isn't impossible physics, it's proof a reservoir was misidentified. Point it at a reported anomaly and it returns what the missing partner would have to be — a mass, a field, a flow, with a required Δu and force.

Pioneer anomalyresult
Solar radiation pressureruled out · A = 6.7
Onboard thermal photonssurvives · needs 3.2%
Later fitted model (2012)3.0% · agree to 7%

Run in 1998 it would not have said “thermal.” It would have said a thermal answer needs 3.2% anisotropy — a number a model can check. Someone built that model fourteen years later.

The real test is the availability leg, A = (F·Δu)/Pavail, not Σ alone — a correction the validation itself forced.
C6 · computedno prior art

The damage–healing number

Self-healing materials have healing rates; nobody had formed the dimensionless ratio. Written down, it reduces to reliability engineering's availability — and to the same Möbius form as thermoelectric ZT.

Ha = kr/kd  →  A = Ha/(1+Ha) = MTBF/(MTBF+MTTR)

The reason nobody wrote it turned out to be experimental, not conceptual: you can't suppress healing while loading — except in vitrimers, gated by temperature. That names the next measurement. And using the law exposed a defect: set ḥ = 0 and it says healing always finishes.

Three systems on one axis; two rows left deliberately empty because the paired data does not exist.

C9 · computeddo not build

The moving coupling point does not beat the bound

The idea that driving a converter's coupling point through the gradient escapes the Kedem–Caplan limit was wrong, and the refutation was inside our own document. Adding motion just adds a conjugate pair; the optimum still collapses to a coefficient.

ZTeff = ZT / (1 + (1−ε)·Pe)  ≤ ZT

It's a Stirling engine with a bad regenerator — and a 2014 paper on rotating thermoelectrics had already found the extra parameter. Twelve years early.

Kept: that one line, saying the regenerator is the whole game — worth more than the device would have been.

Open doors

Questions harvested from findings first filed as losses. Damaging a claim usually produces a better one; the vault had nowhere to put that until now.

Q9

“Fuel-free” names our ignorance, not the physics

Absence of fuel we recognise is not absence of a reservoir. Σ > 1 means a partner was misidentified — and we made that exact error about solar sails before catching it.

Q2

Independent rediscovery is countable

The project keeps re-finding one event. Six instances — and two were the same year (1965/1965), which is a different phenomenon from a 30-year reading gap wearing the same label.

Q1

What decides whether a claim gets checked

Number of groups predicts nothing. Cost and publishability of the check predicts everything. DAMA got two purpose-built refutations; Homestake sat twenty years. Both inputs are public.

Q8

The invisible population

Metrology has a standard way of not resolving disagreements — expansion factors, consensus estimators. G's is 3.9. That population is huge, scheduled, and nearly fame-free.

Method, in one line. Take two fields that describe the same move, measure how many papers cite both, and read what the number means — then try to break your own answer.
